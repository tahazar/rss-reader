# rss-reader

A calm feed reader and read-it-later app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that turns
what you follow into a finite daily edition and delivers it to your e-reader.

Principles: free with no paywalled basics, open source, no server, your own
iCloud for sync, everything processed on device, no telemetry.

- [Scope](docs/SCOPE.md): product principles, feature scope, integration feasibility, serverless architecture, phased plan, decisions and risks.
- [Landscape](docs/LANDSCAPE.md): competitive survey, open-source components to build on, the Kindle opportunity, and the UX thesis.
